CURE

Cancer Undergraduate Research Experience

Your first real step into oncology research.

CURE connects Alfaisal medical students with real oncology research projects led by doctors at Riyadh’s cancer centers. Doctors share what they need, OIG matches them with vetted students, and every project is followed through to the finish.

How CURE works

From a doctor’s idea to a finished outcome, in five steps.

  1. Doctors share a project

    A doctor fills in one short form with the project details and how many students are needed.

  2. Students apply

    An application, then an interview. The selected applicants join our vetted pool.

  3. OIG makes the match

    Each project is paired with the best-fit student, based on interest, availability and skills.

  4. You start working together

    After a quick onboarding, you work directly with your doctor, who supervises the project. Hours and timeline are agreed upfront.

  5. Finish and share the outcome

    Our team checks in along the way. Posters, abstracts and papers are recorded, and both sides give feedback.

What to expect

Clear expectations from the start, so every project runs smoothly for students and doctors.

Time is agreed upfront

Weekly hours and the project timeline are agreed before you begin, so you know exactly what you are signing up for.

Training and ethics

Research ethics, plus any training the hospital or its ethics committee requires before you start.

Confidentiality and professionalism

Patient confidentiality and professional conduct matter at every step of every project.

Clear authorship

Authorship is agreed with your doctor before the project starts, following standard authorship rules. The principal investigator (PI) has a say in it too.

Quick answers

Who can join?

CURE is for Alfaisal medical students.

How much time will it take?

It depends on the project. Weekly hours are agreed upfront between you, your doctor and OIG.

How do doctors take part?

Doctors fill in one short form. OIG finds a vetted student and handles the follow-up.

Where will projects happen?

We are starting with cancer centers in Riyadh, with more partners and cities planned over time.
