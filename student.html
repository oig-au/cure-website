Apply to CURE

Your first real step into oncology research. Tell us about your interests and research experience, and OIG will match you with a project that fits.

Before you start

Have these ready

  • Your research experience, if any (it is fine if you have none yet)
  • Your interests and the project types you prefer
  • Your weekly availability
  • Your CV, ready to upload

What happens next

  • We review your application
  • Selected applicants are invited to an interview
  • Those who pass join our vetted pool
  • OIG matches you with a project based on interest, availability and skills
Open the application The form opens in a new tab. You need to sign in with a Google account to upload your CV.

Questions

Email Oncologyig@alfaisal.edu or follow @Oncoig_au. Are you a doctor with a project? Share it here.

CURE is a program by the Research & Development team of the Oncology Interest Group (OIG), a student group at Alfaisal University.
